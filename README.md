# 🎓 EduConnect — Education Platform with AI Doubt-Solving

> An interactive, multi-lingual education platform connecting **Students**, **Professors**, and **Parents** with **AI step-by-step doubt resolution**, verified faculty escalation, and **Admin governance** in **Telugu (తెలుగు), English, Hindi (हिंदी), and Tamil (தமிழ்)**.

---

## 🌟 What's New & Upgraded

### 🌐 1. 100% Complete Regional Language Conversion (తెలుగు, हिंदी, தமிழ், English)
- When a user selects **Telugu (తెలుగు)** (or Hindi, Tamil, English) from the top navigation bar, the **entire application** converts completely:
  - Navigation tabs, role titles, and badges
  - Card headings, subtitles, and button labels
  - Form placeholders, input prompts, and modal dialogs
  - Empty states, status indicators, and weekly WhatsApp/SMS parent digests
  - AI step-by-step doubt breakdowns formulated in the chosen language!

### 📚 2. Student & Professor Subject Selection
- **Student Subject Management**: Students can choose and update their enrolled subjects for the semester:
  - 📐 Mathematics (గణిత శాస్త్రం)
  - 💻 Computer Science & Coding (కంప్యూటర్ సైన్స్)
  - ⚛️ Physics (భౌతిక శాస్త్రం)
  - 🧪 Chemistry (రసాయన శాస్త్రం)
  - 🧠 Data Science & AI (డేటా సైన్స్)
  - 📚 English & Communication (ఇంగ్లీష్ & కమ్యూనికేషన్)
  - ⚡ Electronics & Systems (ఎలక్ట్రానిక్స్)
- **Professor Subject Specialization**: Professors select their teaching domains when registering and can update them anytime.
- **Subject-Aware Doubts & Classes**: Doubts and classes are tagged by subject, allowing students to ask subject-specific questions that route to specialized faculty!

### 💬 3. Discussion Forum & Peer Study Groups
- Students and professors can participate in peer-learning threads categorized by subject.
- Students can post academic questions, and peers and professors can post replies and moderation feedback in real-time.

### 🔍 4. Find Verified Professors by Subject
- Students and parents can browse and discover faculty members filtered by subject, language, and ratings (⭐ 4.9/5.0).
- Includes one-click action to ask doubts directly to that specific professor.
